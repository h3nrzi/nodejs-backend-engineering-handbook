## Question

What current Iranian requirements can be verified from authoritative sources for an Iran-based individual operating the approved free, invitation-only adult learning pilot, and which applicability questions remain unresolved?

Investigate whether this bounded activity requires a registered entity, permit or registration; relevant handling of account identifiers, private learning records, support communications, retention/deletion and access requests; and data-location or transfer rules. Distinguish actual applicable obligations from proposed laws, voluntary best practice, broad constitutional principles, commercial/payment rules, and institutional or regulated education rules. The pilot provides self-paced content and learner-confirmed completion; it does not award accredited qualifications, take payments, or offer assessed competency claims.

Use current official Persian legislation/regulator sources wherever possible, preserve article numbers and dates, and explain interpretation and coverage limitations. Do not assume an exemption because the pilot is small or free, and do not treat missing evidence as permission. Identify precise follow-up questions for a qualified local source when public primary evidence cannot settle applicability. Do not invent fixed retention periods, localization duties, or a GDPR obligation from the operator/learner market alone.

This research qualifies the owner-confirmed operating arrangement; it does not select infrastructure or establish launch approval. No external contact, account creation, implementation, incorporation, or payment is authorized.

## Answer

### Verified source evidence and limits

Completed the bounded primary-source investigation. [Iranian individual-operator and learner-data obligations](https://github.com/h3nrzi/nodejs-backend-engineering-handbook/blob/408b07f2d0d39cae42cf225451028500c2ba0995/.scratch/learning-platform/research/iran-operator-and-data-obligations.md) preserves the full findings, official texts, article/clause references, retrieval date, inconsistencies and limitations on an unmerged throwaway research branch.

- Retrieved the official promulgation notices and actual full PDFs for the platform privacy directive, its amendment, and the National Data and Information Management Law. The directive contains consent/purpose, minimization, deletion/legal-preservation and encryption provisions. Its legal-person/executive-body preamble and all-service-provider operative clause do not conclusively settle coverage of this individual operator.
- The official amendment changes the original clause 1 implementation window from two to four months, not the scope wording. These are historical windows, not a new grace period for this pilot. The report preserves an adoption-date discrepancy between the original covering letter and attached text rather than silently choosing one.
- The National Data law has a defined public/national-data and sectorally regulated scope. It does not establish that all private learner records are national data, nor a blanket localization duty for this pilot. The separately found personal-data protection proposal is not treated as enacted law.
- WIPO's Electronic Commerce Act reproduction provides relevant personal-data text, but is not a current consolidated Iranian-law assurance. Metadata/text dates and the English penalty cross-reference have unresolved discrepancies. No penalty conclusion or fixed response/retention deadline is drawn from that translation.
- The free, small, invitation-only adult pilot was not shown to be either exempt from or subject to a particular entity, education or digital-publishing permission requirement. Current Criminal Procedure articles 667–670 and their application to a hosted app versus its managed host were not fully authenticated/settled. No universal application-log or learning-history retention period, incident deadline, GDPR duty or permission for foreign transfer is invented.
