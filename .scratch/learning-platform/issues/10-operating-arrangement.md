## Question

Which legal and operational arrangement will manage the pilot serving learners primarily in Iran, and what verified constraints does it impose on service-provider selection and learner data handling?

Decide the managing person or organization, operating country, relevant account and contracting responsibilities, and feasible arrangements to investigate. Do not infer operating jurisdiction from the learner market. Obtain owner input on arrangements actually available to them; do not assume an entity, residence, banking arrangement, or eligibility.

When candidate arrangements are concrete, create scoped research tickets for current jurisdiction-specific obligations and relevant provider availability and eligibility, using authoritative sources. Distinguish provider-account eligibility, accessibility for learners in Iran, and data-location or transfer requirements. Resolve factual dependencies before confirming an arrangement as suitable; record uncertainty and any necessary follow-up explicitly.

This is a planning decision, not a requirement to incorporate an entity, open accounts, purchase services, or deploy. Payment-provider qualification is outside the free initial release and returns only if a later phase introduces payments. No provider is selected by this ticket alone.

Its confirmed constraints inform [Estimate pilot operating costs and approve a spending limit](11-operating-budget.md) and must be available before [Decide the first-release technical boundaries and feasibility](08-first-release-technical-boundaries.md) is finalized.

## Answer

### Arrangement accepted for planning; legal suitability not established

Use the owner-operated, Iran-based individual arrangement as the baseline for product planning, conditional cost estimates and candidate comparison. The owner manages service accounts, provider relationships, expenses within a subsequently approved budget, support and personal-data requests. Available payment methods are Iranian bank accounts/domestic cards in the owner's own name; international payment access is unconfirmed. Prioritize domestic managed-hosting candidates. Hosting geography remains open subject to qualification, with no specific transfer approved.

This resolves the planning choice through the owner's explicit acceptance. It does not establish a licensing exemption, the owner's eligibility for a particular permission/provider, legal suitability, an approved provider, spending approval or launch readiness. The preceding claimed/unresolved checkpoints are historical; this answer supersedes their blanket planning dependency.

### Remaining gates and why they do not block the operator choice

The [legal qualification register](14-individual-pilot-regulatory-clarification.md#legal-qualification-register--verified-text-versus-unsettled-applicability) assigns each remaining question to a specific gate:

- Final record-retention, preservation and irreversible-deletion contracts, and actual provider/data-flow location or transfer commitments, require targeted evidence before their technical decisions can be finalized. [Clarify the individual pilot's licensing and data-rule applicability](14-individual-pilot-regulatory-clarification.md) remains open for those bounded questions and blocks [Decide the first-release technical boundaries and feasibility](08-first-release-technical-boundaries.md), not this operator choice or preliminary costing.
- Activity/licensing classification, individual applicant conditions, general privacy-rule coverage and applicable incident/request deadlines remain explicit pre-launch compliance determinations. They can be recorded in the specification as gates without asserting either exemption or fulfilled compliance. If verified findings require a different operator, permission, provider, scope or cost, reopen this decision and the affected budget/technical decisions before relying on the changed arrangement.
- Proposal status, source date/translation discrepancies not relied on for a current decision, conditional mechanisms and future public-rollout changes remain later verification triggers. They do not justify a generic legal-research dependency on current planning.

### Budget can proceed with conditional estimates

[Estimate pilot operating costs and approve a spending limit](11-operating-budget.md) is now the next frontier decision. It may estimate documented candidate services and obtain the owner's spending preference using explicit assumptions, unknowns and contingencies. An estimate is not qualification: provider-specific individual contracting, accepted domestic billing and relevant service conditions must be evidenced before a paid route is described as feasible. Unverified legal/permission costs must be marked unknown, not silently set to zero. A ceiling does not authorize purchases or cure eligibility.

No other dependency prevents this planning resolution. Material provider qualification and the targeted technical questions remain open at their own gates. No implementation, deployment, purchase, consultation or external communication is authorized.
