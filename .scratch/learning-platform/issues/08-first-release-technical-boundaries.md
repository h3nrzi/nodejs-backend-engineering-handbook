## Question

Which technical boundaries and contracts make the agreed first-release experience feasible with Next.js in client/ and the Handbook's source authority intact?

For selected release capabilities, resolve content ingestion and stable identity, content revisions and links, application and backend responsibilities, learner identity and persistence, and operational requirements. Scope security, accessibility, performance, privacy, and deployment decisions to the approved release. Investigate APIs and provider facts against current documentation; create specific research or prototype tickets for unresolved dependencies before resolving this ticket. Avoid detailed architecture for later phases and avoid building the application.

Translate the selected capabilities from [Choose the course authoring and publishing workflow](03-authoring-and-publishing.md#answer) into enforceable contracts for source revision provenance, consistent preview/approval/publication content identity, approval invalidation, independently selected shared revisions, publication accountability, and preservation of historical publication and learner records. Resolve how publication and recovery enforce current material eligibility and active access removal without silently changing other published courses.

Translate selected capabilities from [Choose the learner journey and evidence of learning](04-learner-experience-and-evidence.md#answer) into contracts for enrollment versus access entitlement, path participation without duplicate course progress, requirement identity and evidence, current fulfillment calculations, historical completion, course/path publication changes and equivalence, saved position, and archive/restore. Enforce meaningful required-work publication minimums and distinguish obstacles from progress. For included assessment and verification capabilities, resolve reviewer authority, evidence correction or invalidation, reassessment, result qualification, feedback availability, and propagation to affected requirements while preserving history. Surface unresolved policy choices explicitly rather than inventing them as implementation details.

Apply [Choose the business and operating constraints for launch](05-business-and-operating-constraints.md#answer). Resolve invitation-only access enforcement independently of enrollment without requiring a dedicated invitation-management system. Specify Persian RTL and course-language behavior, a concrete accessibility standard, and verification criteria appropriate to selected material formats. Define necessary learner data and purposes, private-by-default access, service-provider sharing disclosures, retention periods, account and data deletion, account recovery, and historical-record handling; preservation of history does not authorize indefinite personal-data retention. Obtain owner decisions on unresolved privacy policies and investigate actual jurisdiction-dependent obligations instead of assuming them. Identify concrete accessibility and learner-market availability checks for the eventual release; planning evidence alone is not an operational pilot test.

Final hosting, deployment, and jurisdiction-dependent choices require the confirmed operating arrangement, verified relevant availability and eligibility, cost estimates, and an owner-approved spending limit from the prerequisite decisions. Recheck cost assumptions against the final contracts and reopen the budget decision if necessary. Payment-provider qualification is unnecessary for the free release. Do not resolve this ticket with provider eligibility, operating arrangement, spending approval, or included privacy policies still undecided.


### Selected release boundary

Apply [Define the first release and later product phases](06-first-release-and-phases.md#answer). Select formats and ingestion for externally authored repository-managed text, images, code examples, and written practice. Define enforceable repository/operator publication safeguards without requiring an editor or publisher dashboard. Include course/path revisions, explicit equivalence decisions, preserved historical completion, and in-product explanations; email notifications and a notification center are not required.

Completion uses learner confirmation and undo; prerequisites are advisory. Assessment engines, submission/reviewer workflows, competency claims, mandatory prerequisite verification, video delivery, downloadable attachments, executable exercises, and interactive activities are deferred. Do not require future-phase infrastructure or impose unsupported fulfillment routes.

Multiple independent courses and learning paths are first-release capabilities even if the first invited cohort initially uses only a bounded Handbook course. Support email, operational preview/review/publication, identity/progress isolation across courses, and meaningful multi-course path validation must be covered by the selected contracts and eventual verification approach.

### Experience-review follow-ups

Carry forward the [resolved experience decision](07-first-release-experience-prototype.md#answer), including the owner's prior behavior-level approvals and the [Stitch asset/scope review](../prototypes/experience-review.md). The owner accepted static visual references for discovery; these requirements do not settle technical mechanisms or verify implementation. The experience dependency is resolved; operating-arrangement and budget dependencies remain open.

- Define the contract for uncertain progress-write outcomes: distinguish confirmed state from an operation whose result is unknown, specify the evidence needed before reporting success or definitive failure, reconcile the result safely, and prevent duplicate effects when retrying. Define how the learner sees the last confirmed progress and unresolved intent until reconciliation. Include verification cases for a write that took effect but whose acknowledgment was lost, a write that did not take effect, and repeated retries. Neither earlier model simulations nor the Stitch retry/sync copy verify this contract or select local-first/offline storage.
- Select enforceable authorization, audit-record, and publication-consistency contracts for the [separate repository publisher sequence](../prototypes/publisher-workflow.md). Explain how exact-version preview/review/approval, approval invalidation after edits, explicit publication, source provenance, and current recovery eligibility are enforced. Invitation/access and enrollment confer no publishing authority. No publisher dashboard, mandatory second reviewer, GPG signing, OAuth2 mechanism or public rollout is selected by the static diagram. Illustrative logs/diagnostics establish no authorization, audit integrity or real publication enforcement.
- Translate the [documented screen gaps and scope overrides](../prototypes/experience-review.md#scope-precedence-and-meaningful-gaps) into feasible contracts, including unpictured error/unavailable-content states, saved-position changes, support and privacy/account lifecycle. Current fulfillment uses latest requirements; acknowledgment is not an opt-in revision switch. Preserve history subject to eventual retention/deletion policies, without perpetual-access or unlimited-retention guarantees. Do not implement mockup-only deferred capabilities.
- Plan real learner-control, persistence, responsive/mobile, keyboard, focus and accessibility verification for the implemented release. The owner superseded further visual polishing and browser review of the deleted artifact; static reference acceptance does not pass these future checks. Supply testable criteria to the specification-readiness decision, not another visual-prototype requirement.
