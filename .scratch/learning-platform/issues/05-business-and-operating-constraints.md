## Question

Which business and operating commitments should bound the first release and its rollout?

Decide the initial access and monetization policy, intended launch cohort, languages and accessibility expectations, realistic delivery and operating budget, timeline, and support responsibilities. Identify privacy, data-handling, hosting, and jurisdiction facts that require scoped investigation before selecting solutions. Treat these as open choices; no launch date, price, language, or provider has already been chosen.

Apply the separation between enrollment and access entitlement established in [Choose the learner journey and evidence of learning](04-learner-experience-and-evidence.md#answer). If required human assessment review or external-evidence verification is considered, determine operating capacity, responsibilities, and feasible turnaround expectations before treating it as supported.

## Answer

### Free access within a temporary invited pilot

The first release is an invitation-only pilot. All published courses are free for admitted participants, with no course fees or subscriptions. Enrollment remains a learner's choice to organize studies and track progress; it does not itself grant access entitlement. Pilot admission bounds learner access independently of enrollment.

Invitation-only access is a temporary operating boundary, not a permanent product requirement. Public registration and broader discovery may follow pilot evaluation. Payments, subscriptions, and other monetization models remain possible later; payment-provider qualification is not a first-release dependency.

### Audience, recruitment, and evaluation

Recruit approximately 5–10 adults aged 18 or older from the owner's existing programming community. The count is a provisional target, neither a minimum nor a maximum. Personally managed recruitment, invitations, and coordination are acceptable; a dedicated invitation-management system is not required solely for the pilot. Additional participants may join as support capacity and findings permit.

Evaluate discovery within the admitted experience, enrollment, studying, returning to lessons, and progress tracking. The platform remains a general-purpose learning product despite this community's likely programming focus. These recruitment decisions establish neither actual participants nor learner-validation evidence. The pilot's adult-only audience does not define the permanent age policy or automatically require dedicated age-verification tooling. Detailed evaluation methods and success criteria remain for release planning.

### Language and accessibility

The first-release interface is Persian (Farsi), with mandatory RTL layout support. Each course independently declares its content language; course content is not restricted to Persian. Additional interface languages may follow later.

Accessibility is a release requirement, including keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, semantic structure, and accessible alternatives appropriate to selected material formats. Account for Persian and RTL interfaces. The concrete standard, verification criteria, and delivery contracts must be established during first-release specification; none is selected by this answer.

### Delivery capacity and support

The owner develops the project with AI coding-agent assistance, including Codex. Use approximately 10–15 hours per week as provisional planning capacity, not a fixed commitment. There is no committed launch date. Prioritize manageable scope, low operational complexity, and cost-conscious choices.

The owner initially handles learner support and content, access, and platform reports through one direct channel. Its exact selection remains for release planning. Promise no guaranteed response time during the pilot. Any mandatory human assessment review requires separately confirmed reviewer capacity and communicated turnaround expectations; support ownership establishes neither. Release planning may include only supported fulfillment routes.

### Learner information and historical records

Collect only information necessary for account access, learning continuity, progress tracking, support, and clearly stated pilot evaluation goals. Keep learning records private by default. Avoid advertising trackers, public learner profiles, and unnecessary personal-data collection. Explain what is collected, its purpose, and any sharing with service providers.

Explicitly define retention periods, account deletion, data deletion, account recovery, and historical learning-record treatment before pilot operation. Preservation of learning history must be reconciled with those policies and does not mean indefinite personal-data retention. Product and technical policies remain to be specified, not assumed from this baseline.

### Learner market, operating arrangement, and budget

Iran is the initial target learner market. The legal and operational management country remains undecided and must not be inferred from that market. Future international expansion remains possible.

No monthly operating budget, provider, infrastructure, or operating arrangement is approved. Product planning may proceed while those decisions remain open. Hosting, deployment, and jurisdiction-dependent technical choices cannot be finalized until the operating arrangement is confirmed, relevant provider availability and eligibility are investigated, costs are estimated, and the owner approves a spending limit. Research must use the actual candidate arrangements and services, rather than treating an unknown jurisdiction as a settled fact.
