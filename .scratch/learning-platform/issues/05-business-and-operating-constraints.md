## Question

Which business and operating commitments should bound the first release and its rollout?

Decide the initial access and monetization policy, intended launch cohort, languages and accessibility expectations, realistic delivery and operating budget, timeline, and support responsibilities. Identify privacy, data-handling, hosting, and jurisdiction facts that require scoped investigation before selecting solutions. Treat these as open choices; no launch date, price, language, or provider has already been chosen.
