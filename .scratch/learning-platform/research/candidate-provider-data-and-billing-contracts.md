# Candidate provider data locations and domestic billing contracts

Evidence reviewed 2026-10-17. Bounded research for **Qualify candidate data locations and domestic billing terms**, supporting **Clarify the individual pilot's licensing and data-rule applicability** and **Decide the first-release technical boundaries and feasibility**. This report qualifies public evidence; it selects no supplier and authorizes no account, transfer, purchase, contact or operation.

## Scope and evidence method

The candidate remains Liara managed app/database plus an Iranhost Linux Iran Base receiving mailbox and `.ir` registration. This is the conditional costing scenario from [Verify domestic pilot service costs and billing conditions](https://github.com/h3nrzi/nodejs-backend-engineering-handbook/blob/e0bbc9b9dafcad80382f48f4ccaf58c249576557/.scratch/learning-platform/research/domestic-pilot-service-costs.md). The approved planning ceilings are 3m toman/month, 4m upfront and 3m separately held reserve. The owner confirms a personal device in Iran with at least 5 GB for independent backups, excluding its backup folder from consumer cloud synchronization. Those owner facts are planning inputs, not operational test evidence.

Context7 resolved Liara to `/liara-cloud/docs` and was queried separately for data geography and billing/identity. Iranhost resolution returned no matching library. Primary official docs/product/contract pages were then inspected. Official Liara documentation source was also read at [commit c11c03c19b7bf0e51102b16dceb5105b8e341c47](https://github.com/liara-cloud/docs/tree/c11c03c19b7bf0e51102b16dceb5105b8e341c47). Searches were bounded to these candidates; generic tutorials and unrelated pricing/legal articles were not used as contract evidence.

“Documented” below means a provider representation in public documentation, not a negotiated guarantee, actual configuration or proof of performance. “Unverified” is not a finding that a route is prohibited.

The [privacy policy](https://liara.ir/privacy-policy/), last updated 28 Farvardin 1403, says information is stored in Iran **and may be transferred/accessed anywhere worldwide** for international functions. It describes customer/end-user traffic logging, account/contact/bank data, cookies and support interactions; support/payment/marketing service providers may receive information for their services. No named subprocessor/country list, complete metadata retention schedule or country-restricted staff-access commitment was found there. Its access/transfer reservation prevents treating an Iran data-center listing as an Iran-only processing promise. Contractual permission is also not legal permission for this pilot's actual transfers.

The [Liara terms](https://liara.ir/terms/) require accurate official identity details, permit request inspection/analysis, promise confidentiality subject to legal exceptions, and reserve lawful disclosure. Refunds require owner national-ID/card/IBAN evidence. Account/financial transaction records cannot be deleted under the provider's stated rationale. Customer backups remain the customer's responsibility; infrastructure support excludes application maintenance. Base **account-level** credit exhaustion immediately suspends services and deletes app/DB copies after 24 hours; service feature tiers do not establish a different account level. Minimum payment is 10m rial (1m toman); final invoices apply VAT under current law without stating a percentage. These are provider terms, not independently established statutory retention periods or legal suitability.

Consequently an Iran builder, private DB network and independent domestic export are technically describable candidates, but **a complete Iran-only Liara data-processing contract is not evidenced**. If that guarantee is legally necessary for a particular record, this route cannot be finalized on these public pages alone.

## Iranhost mailbox/domain evidence

The [Linux hosting product page](https://iranhost.com/webhosting/linux-hosting/) advertises Linux Iran Base in an Iran data center, 2 GB hosting, cPanel, POP3/IMAP/Webmail, forwarding, mobile/Outlook sync and spam filtering. Its 275,000-toman monthly equivalent requires annual payment (3.3m cash), and annual free `.ir` is conditional. It does **not explicitly identify the associated mailbox/SMTP/anti-spam storage, backup or support processing countries**. Neither cPanel branding nor Iran website hosting proves those locations. Mailbox-only suitability, encrypted client transport, message-size/quota/export, retention/deletion and forwarding defaults remain to qualify.

The separate [shared-email product](https://iranhost.com/shared-email-hosting/) expressly advertises **Europe** for its mailbox plans. It is an alternative product, not proof that Linux Iran Base mail is in Europe or Iran. No transfer to this alternative is approved. External learner email providers also process messages at their own destinations; choosing a domestic inbox would not keep every email exchange domestic.

The [Iranhost terms](https://iranhost.com/iranhost/rules/) explicitly cover individuals: §2.3 requests “کپی کارت ملی”. §18.2 permits payment “از طریق درگاه‌های آنلاین بانکی موجود در پرتال کاربران” alongside cash/announced accounts. This supports a public domestic bank-gateway route; it proves neither this owner's KYC nor a particular card/gateway transaction. §§6.6/“رویه حفاظت” promise confidentiality/non-disclosure with judicial exceptions; no country-bound staff/subprocessor commitment is given. §17 puts independent backups on customers, advertises weekly file copies held up to one week, disclaims their correctness, and leaves archive extraction potentially chargeable. It does not specifically guarantee mailbox coverage or backup country. Expiry gives no data-retention entitlement. No product-specific tax rate/inclusion was established.

The initial [domain tariff](https://iranhost.com/domain/) is the prior report's conditional 95,000 toman/year; IRNIC handle/registration and exact renewal remain separate gates. No domain name was queried, ordered or reserved. Registry/customer identity and public domain/DNS data require their own disclosure assessment; learner records need not be put in registration fields. This report establishes no complete IRNIC eligibility or data-processing contract.

## Billing and budget gaps

Liara's [credit-increase documentation](https://docs.liara.ir/references/console/increase-credit/) documents entering **rial** and opening a payment page. Its [account documentation](https://docs.liara.ir/references/console/see-user-info/) covers natural-person name, phone, national/universal code; the privacy policy says registration uses identity verification. These support individual-account handling, but the reviewed text does not name a current accepted domestic card network/gateway or complete required KYC documents. Generic bank-payment tutorials do not fill that gap. Iranhost's explicit gateway clause is stronger evidence, while actual availability/owner acceptance remains untested.

No newly verified PaaS/DB traffic allowance, export-traffic price, extra-disk tariff, exact current invoice VAT percentage, mailbox annual tax treatment or renewal total was obtained. Preserve the prior modest core **1.7m/month** as a conditional subtotal with **1.3m** monthly headroom; preserve mailbox/domain cash subtotal **3.395m** and **605k** upfront headroom. Neither remainder proves fit. Do not import IaaS traffic pricing, invent zero-rated exports or spend the reserve as an overrun allowance. A current itemized tax-inclusive quote/terms must cover the selected products, backup/export work and actual calendar billing before final feasibility.

## Exact handoff gates

1. **Geography/access:** identify runtime/DB, builder, image registry/cache, app/security logs, provider backups, mailbox/SMTP/anti-spam, support/billing and each material upstream recipient. Obtain applicable country/access/deletion commitments or exclude the affected route. Provider-wide transfer reservations cannot be erased by owner acceptance.
2. **Domestic independent copy:** owner facts now establish the candidate in Iran outside hosting and cloud sync. Technical planning still must establish encryption/key separation, local device access protection, secure export/automation/failure warnings, retained generation sizes within 5 GB, legal holds/deletion reconciliation and verified restoration. Device location/storage is no recovery-readiness evidence; new costs reopen the budget.
3. **Supplier feasibility:** verify individual KYC, accepted owner bank/card route, binding product/term availability, mailbox workflow/export, domain/renewal and an all-in invoice/metering contract within approved envelopes. No onboarding or purchase was performed here.
4. **Legal applicability:** public provider promises are not national-data/sectoral law. This bounded provider pass found no authoritative provision newly establishing that this actual free invited adult pilot must keep every copy/access in Iran, and establishes no unrestricted foreign-transfer entitlement. The precise legal applicability determination remains in **Clarify the individual pilot's licensing and data-rule applicability**, using the actual flows above. No broad licensing search was repeated. Category 2 privacy/activity/incident pre-launch duties retain their existing gate.

The research question is answered with explicit evidence boundaries. Exact selected-service geography/access and billing contracts remain qualification work; this report must not be converted into approval of a complete feasible supplier bundle.
