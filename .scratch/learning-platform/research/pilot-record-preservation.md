# Pilot record preservation: host and application roles

Evidence retrieved 2026-10-17. Bounded AFK research for **Establish record-specific preservation and deletion constraints**. This report supplies primary text and the limits of its application; it does not establish general legal clearance or select a supplier.

## Result for the technical decision

The official enactment text distinguishes access providers, domestic hosts, and any person possessing or controlling data. A domestic host's own customer records, hosted content and change traffic have different minimum periods. The application operator can receive a preservation order regardless of whether the application is classified as a host. An owner-device backup remains within that possession/control analysis. Using managed hosting does not transfer every application duty to the supplier. [Criminal Procedure additions, articles 667–670](https://rc.majlis.ir/fa/law/show/913396)

The complete official regulation is now recovered, including definitions 1(a)–(c) and articles 2–6. Its hosting definition is broad enough that this application's provision of persistent learner records needs an actual role determination; no authoritative pilot-specific exclusion was found. Therefore the final application record schedule, identity/address collection consequences and unconditional irreversible-erasure promise remain unqualified. This is an exact classification/current-technical-rule gap, not a reason to reopen generic operator/licensing research. [Electronic-evidence regulation](https://rc.majlis.ir/fa/law/show/897504)

## Candidate facts used

The conditional cost model is an Iran-based individual running a free invited adult pilot; potential Liara app/managed database, possible Iranhost receiving inbox and .ir registration, with independent encrypted copies on a personal owner-controlled device. During this research the owner confirmed that copies are modeled on a device in Iran and that its backup folder is excluded from consumer cloud sync. This settles that modeling input only. No running system, supplier contract, upload capability, actual storage inventory or working backup process was inspected. Ten learners, a 1 GB database allowance and 5 GB backup capacity are estimates, not legal limits or evidence of reliability.

## Exact provisions: faithful English reading

English descriptions below are research paraphrases, not an authoritative translation. Persian provision numbers identify the controlling text at the linked official source.

### Criminal Procedure articles 667–670

- **667:** Access providers retain traffic data for at least six months **after creation**, and user information for at least six months **after subscription termination**. Note 1 defines traffic as data generated by systems along the communications chain enabling origin-to-destination tracing, including origin, route, date/time, duration, volume and service type. Note 2 includes service/technical/duration information, identity, geographic/postal or IP address, telephone and other personal details. These are provider-role records; “user information” cannot simply be equated with every educational outcome. [Official text, 667 and notes](https://rc.majlis.ir/fa/law/show/913396)
- **668:** Domestic hosting providers retain **their users' information** for at least six months after subscription termination, and stored content plus traffic arising from changes for at least fifteen days. These are minimums, not maximums or automatic erasure dates. Unlike 667 traffic, the article does not expressly state “after creation” for the fifteen-day clause; do not invent a universal clock for all content or count fifteen days from learner account closure. [Official text, 668](https://rc.majlis.ir/fa/law/show/913396)
- **669:** A judicial authority may order preservation of stored computer data needed for investigation/proceedings by any person having possession or control. Urgent judicial-officer orders are possible where damage/change/loss threatens; the officer must inform the judicial authority within 24 hours. This is not a 24-hour response allowance for the app. The provision penalizes refusal and prohibited disclosure/tipping off. Note 1 separates preservation from provision/disclosure. Note 2 sets an initial preservation duration up to three months, extendable by judicial order; it is not an automatic three-month deletion right. [Official text, 669](https://rc.majlis.ir/fa/law/show/913396)
- **670:** Judicial provision orders may require the persons identified in 667–669 to provide protected data to judicial officers; refusal and failure to retain/care for it attract the referenced sanction. Preservation authority alone does not authorize general disclosure. [Official text, 670](https://rc.majlis.ir/fa/law/show/913396)

### Complete regulation definitions 1(a)–(c)

The [official regulation, article 1](https://rc.majlis.ir/fa/law/show/897504) defines:

- **1(a), access provider:** persons enabling users' connection to domestic/international computer, telecommunications or communications networks, or another independent network; examples include suppliers, distributors and sellers of network-access services. Serving lessons over someone else's Internet connection does not by itself establish that activity.
- **1(b), hosting provider:** persons granting users access to space created by computer/telecommunications/communications systems under their possession or control, so data can be stored or processed, by the provider itself or requesting user, for retention, publication, distribution, provision on domestic/international networks **or any other purpose**. It is not limited in the wording to infrastructure resellers, paid services, public publishing or arbitrary file uploads.
- **1(c), provision of electronic data:** making all or part of data retained/preserved by access/hosting providers **or other possessors/controllers** available. This definition does not make every possessor a hosting provider; it confirms the wider provision category.

Two short Persian anchors capture the role distinction: article 1(b), «ذخیره یا پردازش کنند»; article 1(c), «تحت تصرف یا کنترل دارند». [Official regulation](https://rc.majlis.ir/fa/law/show/897504)

### Regulation articles 2–6, including both article 5 notes

| Provision | Complete substantive requirement and consequence |
| --- | --- |
| **2** | Access/hosting providers must use systems capable of retaining traffic and user information in accordance with former Computer Crimes Act 32–33. This is a capability requirement, not an application-independent six-month period. |
| **3 + note** | Access systems must record communications passing through them and retain their own/related users' traffic for six months after creation. The note specifically requires in-person Internet vendors/cafes to keep daily identity, address, start/end time and allocated IP records. The cafe rule is not evidence that this pilot must collect identity documents. |
| **4** | Access providers retain user information at least six months after subscription termination or cancellation; identity and address must be in the contract. This consequence requires the actual access-provider role. |
| **5 + note 1** | Domestic hosts and domestic representatives of foreign hosts retain their users' information at least six months after subscription termination; stored content/change traffic at least fifteen days. Subscription forms must identify users and their addresses. Systems must store every content change, including modification/deletion and the resulting traffic. Destructive overwrite or a current-only snapshot cannot be presumed sufficient. Exact event clock, version representation and implementation are not prescribed here. |
| **5 note 2** | Accumulation/temporary caching of information **in providing access services** does not make the person a host. Persistent learning history is not shown to be this narrow exception. |
| **6** | Access/hosting providers and preservation executors must secure retained/protected traffic and content under rules/instructions approved by the Head of Judiciary. An encryption choice alone does not prove conformity with all issued instructions. |

Source for every row: [official regulation, articles 2–6](https://rc.majlis.ir/fa/law/show/897504). The old-law references remain in the regulation's wording. The later code reproduces the corresponding subject matter in 667–670; article 698 repeals old numbered procedural provisions. This report does not silently edit the regulation's cross-references or claim that their formal consolidation is complete. [Code, 698–699](https://rc.majlis.ir/fa/law/show/913396)

### Related technical and order rules actually recovered

Regulation **7–10** require preservation of integrity, confidentiality, validity and nonrepudiation; searchable/reportable obligatory records; coordination to official time; and annual technical procedures/security guidance from the judiciary IT/statistics center with the communications ministry. The currently issued procedures were not located. **11–16** cover any stored data including encrypted/deleted/hidden data, pending judicial confirmation of urgent orders, an explicit data/subject/duration order, immediate execution after service and an execution record. **17–19** specify explicit provision orders, protected delivery and signed handover records. No generic provider backup service is certified as satisfying these rules. [Official regulation, 7–19](https://rc.majlis.ir/fa/law/show/897504)

The supplied facts support a serious possible hosting classification; neither an exemption nor a blanket classification has authoritative support. 2. **Current instruments:** verify any later amendment affecting 667–670/1–6 and applicable issued technical instructions under 6/10. Exact current penalty amounts are outside the contract and unverified; do not quote the original fine as current. Failed searches are not proof of absence.
3. **Provider implementation evidence:** for each selected host/mailbox, qualify customer/content/change-log retention, legal holds, end-of-subscription event, restore/export/deletion behavior and copies outside owner visibility. A statutory duty is not evidence the candidate implements it or that its backups recover the pilot.

A general design for classified records, restricted preservation copies, hold overrides, confidential order handling and recoverable storage can be discussed now, labeled provisional.
