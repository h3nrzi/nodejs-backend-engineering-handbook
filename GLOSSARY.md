# Learning platform

A general-purpose learning product for courses across different subjects, audiences, and experience levels.

## Language

**Learning platform**:
The product through which learners access courses and educational materials, follow learning paths, practice, and track their learning. Its scope spans multiple subjects and experience levels.

**Handbook**:
The Node.js Backend Engineering Handbook, the platform's first educational content source and initial use case.

**Learner progress**:
A learner's recorded advancement through a structured educational journey. It is distinct from the learning outcomes of a course and does not by itself demonstrate competency.

**Course learning outcome**:
What a learner is intended to know or be able to do through a particular course. Learning outcomes belong to individual courses rather than defining the platform's subject or audience.

**Self-paced learning**:
Independent study in which learners choose when to start, continue, and progress through a course without depending on a fixed schedule or instructor availability.
