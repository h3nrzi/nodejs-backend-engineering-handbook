## Gaps found and resolved in author review

- Initial sequence diagram failed Mermaid parsing because semicolons in sequence text were treated as separators. Labels were rewritten; final exact sources were validated and rendered again.
- Retry fixture intentionally permits independent creates for one seat to isolate duplication. Added an explicit chapter qualification that it does **not** implement the earlier uniqueness promise.
- Parsing fixture checks fixed small inputs, not streaming limits or duplicate-member interoperability. Added the explicit 1 KiB/tool-support boundary rather than claiming an unrun integration.
- Kept the historical discovery record and preflight intact; added a dated supersession notice instead of rewriting old pending results as current approval.

No required mechanism remains `absent`, `named` or only `explained` after this review. SOAP/OData/federation remain intentionally named **signposts**, as required by CD06, outside C01's deep-mechanism claims. Production authentication, durable replay, storage isolation, full schema/HTTP integration and learner acceptance remain unverified; none is manufactured to close an editorial row.
